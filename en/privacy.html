Privacy Policy

Takyyat (v2.0) · Last updated: 2 November 2026

Warif ("we", "us") provides Takyyat, an online party-games platform. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

  • Account and profile: nickname, optional photo, player ID, and your email if you sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Content you create: in-game text chat, player reports and support messages. The app has no voice chat.
  • Gameplay and usage: games and rooms you join, results, rewards, coin and gem balances, and how you interact with app screens.
  • In-app purchase history: the coins and gems you bought and when. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Technical data: device type, operating system, app version, crash and performance reports.
  • Identifiers: device ID, and Apple's advertising identifier if you allow tracking.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, or sensitive health or financial data.

2. How we use your data

  • To run games, rooms, friends, the wallet and rewards.
  • To keep players safe: filter abusive content, handle reports, and prevent cheating and fraud.
  • To fix crashes, improve the app and understand how it is used.
  • To show ads and measure their performance; ads are personalised only if you allow tracking.
  • To respond to you and comply with applicable law.

3. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share the minimum necessary with service providers acting on our behalf under data-protection obligations, in these categories: cloud hosting and databases; usage analytics; crash and performance reporting; advertising and install measurement; advertising networks; and the sign-in and payment services provided by the platforms (Apple and Google). We may disclose data where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of players.

4. Advertising and tracking

After the home screen we show a short explanation, then Apple's official tracking prompt. If you decline, the app works fully and your data is not used for personalised advertising across other companies' apps and websites. You can change your choice at any time in your device settings.

5. International transfers

Your data may be processed and stored on servers outside your country of residence. We use contractual and technical safeguards to protect it in line with applicable law.

6. Retention and security

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we remove your personal data from our active systems; we may retain limited records where necessary to meet legal obligations, prevent fraud or resolve disputes. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, the GDPR and the CCPA), you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to its processing, or withdraw consent. The easiest way to delete is Profile → "Delete account" in the app, which removes your account and data from our servers and revokes your Sign in with Apple link. For any other request, email us below.

8. Age requirement

Takyyat is intended for users aged 13 and over, or the higher minimum age in your country, and is not directed at children. We do not permit accounts for anyone below that age. If we learn that an account belongs to a user under the permitted age, we will suspend it and delete its data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us data, contact us and we will review and delete it promptly.

9. Community safety

Chat is text only and subject to abuse filtering. Any player can be reported or blocked from their profile card, and our team reviews reports.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with its date and notify you of any material change.
